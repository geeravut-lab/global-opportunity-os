# Impact metrics — proposed definitions

North-star hypothesis: distinct people reaching a verified useful outcome, with equitable access and sustainable delivery cost. Signups, AI chats and notifications are activity, not impact. No baseline or actual outcome exists yet.

## Measurement specification

| Metric | Numerator / denominator or rule | Evidence / owner |
|---|---|---|
| Verified outcome people | Distinct pseudonymous persons with an approved verified outcome in period | Outcome reviewer; do not sum across programs as unique people |
| Referral completion | Referrals reaching confirmed service receipt / submitted referrals in a fixed submission cohort | Operations; report pending, withdrawn and lost-to-follow-up separately |
| Time to first response | Median and p90 elapsed time from submission to first human acknowledgment | Referral events; distinguish calendar and staffed hours |
| Time to service | Submission to confirmed receipt, by cohort | Operations; pending cases remain censored, not zero |
| Listing accuracy | Audited active listings matching current provider evidence / active listings sampled | Independent directory reviewer |
| Cost per verified outcome | Attributable delivery/verification/support cost / verified outcomes | Finance + impact; report total vs marginal cost |
| Equitable access | Completion/wait/abandonment rates by safely collected optional cohort | Privacy-reviewed analyst; suppress small cohorts |
| Complaints and harm | Safety reports, substantiated incidents, response time and unresolved severe cases | Safeguarding lead; lower reporting alone is not success |
| User experience | Optional accessible feedback after delivery, with response rate | Research lead; never condition service on praise |
| Paying buyer retention | Renewed eligible organizations / organizations up for renewal | Finance; actual contracts, not intent |

## Evidence ladder

Activity recorded → user self-report → provider attestation → corroborated receipt → independently reviewed verified outcome. Define category-specific evidence: e.g. training attendance is not employment; job acceptance is not wage receipt; referral sent is not medical treatment. Use proportionate checks and avoid collecting medical or wage documents by default. A disputed outcome is excluded from verified totals until independent review. Keep confidence/evidence level visible.

Deduplicate by stable person/referral/program identifiers, not names alone. Count one latest valid outcome per defined outcome event; preserve correction history. Display reporting window, cohort definition, follow-up duration, denominator, missingness, exclusions and evidence level. Distinct people across organizations require privacy-preserving authorized linkage, not exporting identities to sponsors.

## Later employment/learning metrics

When those modules exist: active earners with confirmed pay; total worker earnings separated from fees/GMV; median within-person income change using consented baseline/follow-up; repeat employment in a defined 90-day window; employer retention; cost per verified placement; skill completion versus actual employment. Do not infer causal income improvement from a before/after comparison alone. Comparison groups or stronger evaluation require separate ethical/research design.

## Anti-gaming and privacy

Monitor hard-to-serve access, wait times and unmet needs so teams are not rewarded for selecting only easy cases. Separate sponsor funding from case verification. Report negative and null results. Proposed small-cell suppression n<10 includes complementary suppression and restrictions on repeated overlapping filters/exports; a threshold alone does not guarantee anonymity. Aggregate reports omit exact addresses, narratives and identifiers. Sensitive cohort collection is optional, purpose-specific and not necessary for access.
