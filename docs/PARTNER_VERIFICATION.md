# Partner verification and trust operations — proposed

A badge means specific checks completed for a defined scope and time; it never guarantees safety or outcomes. NGO, employer, school and care-service checks differ. Being a sponsor does not grant a badge.

## Lifecycle

Draft → submitted → under_review → verified / rejected. Verified → expired / suspended. Rejected, expired or suspended may seek re-review; only an independent reviewer can reinstate. Store decisions, scope, sources, reviewer, conflicts and expiry. Proposed normal recheck every 6 months, sooner on complaints or material changes; actual cadence requires capacity approval.

## Review checklist

1. Validate legal identity/registration against an appropriate official source when available; do not accept a logo or uploaded certificate alone.
2. Independently confirm contact and representative authority using a source outside the submitted form.
3. Check actual program, area, eligibility, service language, accessibility, capacity, costs, safe contact and complaint channel.
4. For employers: inspect wage transparency, payment process, worker protections, no recruitment deposits and prohibited/unsafe offers. No employment of minors through MVP.
5. For child-facing providers: require locally reviewed safeguarding policy, staff controls and incident route; ordinary organization verification is insufficient.
6. For care/regulated services: verify applicable authorization with qualified local review. Listing does not endorse clinical advice.
7. Execute data-sharing terms: purpose, fields, onward disclosure, retention, incident duties, safe contacts and deletion/withdrawal cooperation.
8. Record reviewer conflicts and independent approval; no self-approval or paid fast-track bypass.

Community groups lacking formal registration can use a documented alternative trust pathway: independent references, site/service validation, limited program scope and enhanced oversight approved by the local operator. Never falsely label this as official registration verification or bypass regulated-service requirements.

## Listing freshness and publication

Each listing needs an accountable owner, verified source, last checked date, expiry/refresh date, eligibility version, geography, costs, capacity and referral route. Proposed freshness window 30 days for ordinary listings, shorter for volatile aid/jobs; enforce the earlier of program closing date, listing expiry and partner verification expiry. Changed eligibility, fees, contact or delivery area returns to review. Translation review must track the same source version.

Suspension blocks new referrals and recommendations immediately; staff assess existing cases for safe continuation/transfer with fresh consent. Do not delete evidence or automatically broadcast complaint details to the accused organization. Offer reasoned appeal to an independent reviewer; publish honest status without revealing reporters. Appeals do not automatically restore access.

Proposed review target 5 staffed business days; high-severity reports immediate triage during published service hours. These are capacity hypotheses, not promises of round-the-clock coverage. Track backlog and pause onboarding if safe review capacity is exceeded.
