# User journeys — proposed

## Adult direct access

1. Choose language and see actual local coverage; browse without registering.
2. Select needs and approximate area; skip sensitive questions. Manual search always available.
3. Review suggestions with why-matched explanation, source, date, costs, eligibility and limitations.
4. Choose a current offer. Create a minimal account only to track a referral; external self-application may remain account-free.
5. Preview named recipient and exact shared fields, choose safe contact, explicitly submit.
6. Partner acknowledges, accepts or declines with understandable reason; user sees next step and can withdraw.
7. User and partner attest service delivery separately. A disputed outcome stays disputed, excluded from verified totals pending human review.

No results: explain coverage gap and offer manual search or staffed assistance; never fabricate an offer. Full/expired listing at submit: stop and offer current alternatives, preserving the draft without sending it elsewhere.

## Assisted older adult / user with disability

A trained community helper signs in as themselves, explains the process in accessible language, verifies scoped authority and records the user's safe preferences. The user reviews sharing through an accessible channel. The helper sees only authorized tasks. Revocation removes future access immediately. A helper cannot change payout/contact details without separate verified authority; MVP has no payout handling.

## Child or young person

Public education listings are browseable without a child account. When age screening indicates a minor or age cannot be safely established for a gated workflow, stop personal profiling and referrals, avoid retaining typed sensitive content, and show age-appropriate public information. A trained local partner route can be offered only if approved and verified. No minor employment marketplace, open messages or AI diagnosis. After P-09 approval, build a separate guardian/assent journey with exceptions reviewed by safeguarding staff, not borrowed adult forms.

## Partner onboarding and listing

An organization submits legal/alternative community evidence, contact and program coverage. Reviewer independently checks authority and service details. Approval has scope and expiry. Staff publish only reviewed listings; material edits trigger re-review. Suspension hides affected offers and pauses pending handoffs; operations contact existing referred users through safe channels where necessary. Reinstatement requires independent review and appeal record.

## Caseworker and sponsor

Caseworker opens assigned referrals only, confirms capacity and requests additional fields through a new consent step. State changes append events. Sponsor opens an aggregate report with definitions, dates, evidence quality and suppressed cohorts; no case names, exact locations or downloadable person-level data.

## Withdrawal, correction and safety report

User withdraws sharing; unsent handoffs stop, partner gets a documented deletion/cessation request where appropriate, and the interface explains what was already sent. User requests export/deletion after identity verification without exposing another person's records. Reports may be anonymous; no forced contact with the reported helper or partner. Serious safety reports reach the named safeguarding queue; automated moderation is only a triage aid.
