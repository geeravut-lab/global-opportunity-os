# Project vision

Confirmed mission (C-01–C-03): create a sustainable social web platform helping underserved people worldwide, across all beneficiary groups, reach opportunities that improve their lives.

Proposed positioning: **AI Opportunity Navigator + Verified Partner Network + Impact Tracking.** Help a person understand options, find a real program, take a consented next step and learn whether support was actually delivered. The moat to validate is reliable local delivery and trusted data, not a chatbot alone.

## Who the platform serves

People facing poverty or unemployment need fair paid opportunities and essential services. People with disabilities need accessible interactions, accommodations and support without compelled diagnosis disclosure. Older adults need plain language, safe assisted use and care/benefit navigation. Children and youth need verified education and protected age-appropriate pathways. Caregivers need flexible work and support. Informal workers and small producers need skills and fair market access. Displaced and disaster-affected people need current verified information without dangerous location or status exposure.

These are overlapping needs, not labels to assign automatically. A user may select multiple goals and decline sensitive questions. No group is promised every service at launch. Publish actual coverage by place, language and program category.

## Proposed product principles

Essential access free; dignity before conversion; assistance without forced AI; no fabricated opportunities; humans accountable for high-stakes decisions; minimum personal data; verified outcomes over signups; accessible and multilingual by design; transparent conflicts of interest; local operating readiness before expansion.

Long-term ecosystem: work/income, education/skills, care referrals, benefits navigation, inclusive commerce, NGO case coordination, employer/CSR services and impact reporting. Financial inclusion can be a regulated-partner referral later, never an implicit lending license.

## MVP hypothesis

If local partners keep listings accurate and receive well-scoped referrals, a guided directory plus case tracking can reduce search effort and improve confirmed access to services enough for organizations to pay. Validate with users and partner operations; do not assume AI increases outcomes or that user volume creates revenue automatically.

## Boundaries

The platform is not an emergency dispatcher, employer of every listed worker, medical diagnostic service, lender, custody wallet or guarantee of benefits. State operational limits in context, with usable next steps and verified local contact information. Do not burden every screen with generic warnings.
